    Install this app on your phone or computer

    Install Property Desk once. After that it opens with one tap from your home screen, like any other app, and it also works without internet.

    Android (Chrome)

    1. Open this page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right.
    3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

    iPhone (Safari)

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    Computer (Chrome or Edge)

    1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu.
    2. Choose Install Property Desk.

    Your houses are saved on this phone or computer only. Colleagues have their own separate lists. Use Download Excel now and then to keep a backup.
